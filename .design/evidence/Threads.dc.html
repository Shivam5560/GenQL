Title the thread, don’t quote it

Ask the model for a ≤40-character noun phrase once, on the first turn, and store it as ThreadSummary.title. It already exists as a field — it is just being filled with the question. One extra call per thread, never per turn.

Group by day, drop the pager

last_active_at is already on the wire. Day headings give the list a shape that eight identical rows never had, and they cost nothing. Prev/Next then has no job: a filter field is the control people reach for at this size.

Mark only what is actionable

Two dots, no more: brand for a thread whose gate is still holding a question, red for one that stopped. A finished thread gets no dot — that is the normal case and does not need marking.
